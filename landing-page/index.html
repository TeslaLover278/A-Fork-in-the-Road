How it works

Real navigation stays real navigation. The comedy rides shotgun.

01

Set a destination

Search an address or place, same as any maps app. Fork in the Road calculates a live route and starts tracking your GPS position immediately.

02

Drive, get real directions

Turn-by-turn instructions are announced at the right moment, every time. If you miss a turn, it reroutes automatically — no different from any other nav app.

03

Get heckled the whole way

Between instructions, Dan and Harry banter about the drive — at start, before turns, during reroutes, on arrival, and occasionally out of nowhere on a long straightaway. Their commentary never delays or replaces a real instruction.

Meet the cast

Two voices. Zero navigational expertise. Strong opinions anyway.

Dan

“Recalculating. That word has never once meant anything good.”
😏

Harry

The smug one

Has never once been wrong, according to Harry. Backseat-drives with total, unearned confidence.

“And that, Dan, is how it's done.”

As chatty as you want

A frequency toggle in Settings goes from Off — real directions only — to Occasional — commentary at the big moments — to Chatty — near-constant bickering, including idle chatter on long straightaways. Mute either character individually, any time.
